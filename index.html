About the data

Pub discovery uses Google Places (New). Ratings and review counts are from Google when available. Searches geographic tiles and displays up to 20 matching venues ranked by the selected sort order and may include bars, not only traditional pubs. Sports suitability, meal service and outdoor seating use Google attributes when available. Review-based atmosphere and beer tags are rough estimates from a limited Google review sample; actual tap lists and live fixtures are not verified; otherwise use the linked venue website or review guides. Google venue information is provided by Google Places. CAMRA WhatPub and sports guides are linked for independent checking; their databases are not imported. Links to third-party guides are searches, not claims of endorsement or verified reviews.
